How fast is nationwide delivery in South Africa?

Standard door-to-door courier delivery takes 2 to 4 business days nationwide. Express overnight shipping options are available upon request during checkout.

How do I choose the right padel racket shape?

Round rackets (like our Precision Control Frame) offer a larger sweet spot and low balance for control. Diamond rackets (like our Attack Power Smash) are top-heavy and built for high power hitters.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We accept exchanges and returns within 30 days of purchase, provided the items are unworn, in original packaging, and tags remain attached.

How should I care for Cabretta leather golf gloves?

Avoid wringing or pulling heavily on the leather when wet. Allow the glove to air dry flat away from direct sunlight to maintain soft elasticity and tactile grip.
