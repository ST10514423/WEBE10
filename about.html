About Fairway Finds

Our History

Founded in 2024, Fairway Finds began with a passion for bringing high-quality sports apparel and specialized equipment to athletes across South Africa. What started as a boutique sourcing operation has grown into a premier online hub for golf enthusiasts and racket sport players.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver a seamless e-commerce experience connecting sports enthusiasts with curated apparel and high-performance gear designed for durability and style.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading direct-to-consumer online sports retailer, recognized for exceptional product standards and dedicated customer service.
